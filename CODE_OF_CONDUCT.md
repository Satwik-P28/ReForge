# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ReForge a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing technical honesty about limitations and evidence

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, sexualized language, and publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers via the contact listed in SECURITY.md. Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings or bans.
